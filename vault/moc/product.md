# Product — Map of Content

Canonical product documents, in precedence order:

1. [Product definition](../../docs/product/PRODUCT.md) — customer, promise, scope, and success measures.
2. [Behavioral specification](../../docs/product/BEHAVIOR.md) — observable rules independent of implementation.
3. [Phase 1 acceptance](../../docs/product/PHASE-1-ACCEPTANCE.md) — minimum scenarios and release gates.
4. [Decision log](../../docs/product/DECISIONS.md) — accepted and proposed technical decisions.

## Guardrails

- Vault notes do not change product scope.
- User-visible changes update behavior and acceptance documents.
- Meaningful technical commitments are accepted or superseded in the decision log.
- Deferred ideas do not become Phase 1 requirements without an explicit product decision.
